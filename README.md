**PFine** is an offline-first, relational desktop workspace designed for non-governmental organisations (NGOs), field agencies, enterprise teams, and project managers. Built to operate seamlessly without continuous internet connectivity, PFine links projects, beneficiaries, geographic records, field activities, photo evidence, and document archives into unified story graphs and dossiers.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Command Centre & Entity Explorer
* **Live System Metrics:** Real-time metrics across projects, duties, beneficiaries, geographical records, documents, photo vaults, and imported datasets.
* **Connected Dossiers & Story Graphs:** View interconnected relationships between beneficiaries, field staff, locations, and project milestones in a visual graph interface.

### 📁 Project Management & Field Item Distribution
* **Sub-Item & Inventory Tracking:** Monitor item distribution, active vs. destroyed equipment, and survival/retention rates across field operations.
* **Budget & Custom Data:** Track budget allocations, operational costs, project status, custom JSON fields, and tagging.

### 📍 Beneficiaries, Administrative Hierarchy & Spatial Maps
* **Location Mapping:** Hierarchical classification by County, Sub-County, Ward, and Village levels.
* **Spatial Records:** Store spatial coordinate matrices, static map snapshots, and Google Maps embed snippets directly within beneficiary dossiers.

### 📑 Excel Importer & Evidence Vault
* **Spreadsheet Mapping:** Parse and import `.xls` / `.xlsx` files into reviewable, entity-linked rows.
* **Tagged Photo Vault:** Organize field evidence with pre-categorized tags (*Before*, *During*, *After*, *Training*, *Follow-up*).
* **Document Management:** Centralized offline storage for contracts, field reports, compliance forms, and certificates.

### 🗓️ Activities, Duties & Timeline
* **Field Logs & Task Duties:** Assign duties, track activity timelines, and record custom JSON field metrics.
* **Interactive Calendar:** Plan monthly touchpoints, field visits, and audit schedules.

### 🛡️ Offline Relational Architecture & Customization
* **100% Offline Capability:** Powered by a local desktop relational database (`pfine-organisation-project-management.db`) ensuring data privacy and zero cloud downtime.
* **White-Label Branding:** Customize organizational branding, operational settings, and demo data setups.
* **Report Centre:** Export structured CSV datasets and printable reporting suites.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Download & Install
1. Visit the [Pfine Download Page](https://www.pfine360.com/pfine-organisation-project-management/).
2. Download the latest Windows installer: `pfine-organisation-project-management-setup-win10-win11-0.1.0.exe`.
3. Launch the installer and follow the on-screen installation wizard.

### 2. Workspace Setup
1. Launch **PFine** from your desktop or Start Menu.
2. Complete the initial login setup.
3. (Optional) Go to **Settings > White-Label Settings** to upload your organization logo, name, and custom branding assets.

### 3. Importing Existing Data
1. Navigate to **Excel Importer** in the navigation drawer.
2. Select your project or beneficiary spreadsheet (`.xls` / `.xlsx`).
3. Map the columns to PFine database fields and confirm import.
